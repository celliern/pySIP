# Stochastic state-space inference and prediction

**pySIP** models continuous-time linear stochastic systems from discrete, noisy
measurements. It combines physical grey-box models with temporal Gaussian
processes and supports frequentist and Bayesian parameter inference.

## Get started

- [Quickstart](content/quickstart.md): prepare data, configure a model, fit its
  parameters, and generate predictions.
- [Advanced usage](content/advanced.md): parameter coordinates, transforms,
  priors, model composition, and custom models.
- [Cookbook](cookbook.md): Gaussian-process and thermal-network notebooks,
  including their saved results and downloadable sources.
